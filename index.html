Kape means coffee. Dayo means guest.

That is the whole idea. Kape Dayo is a neighborhood cafe where whoever walks in gets a seat and a good cup.

Come for an iced latte on a hot afternoon, a frappe to share, or a hot americano and a pastry on the way to work.
